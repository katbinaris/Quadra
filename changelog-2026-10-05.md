**Quadra: real MIDI 🎹**

MIDI mode now actually sends MIDI. Pick a synth, turn the knob, and the synth follows.

**What you get**
- **Synth profiles** built from each maker's own MIDI documentation:
  - **KORG minilogue xd**: 48 parameters at the synth's full 10-bit resolution, all 500 programs
  - **Roland JU-06A**: 33 parameters
  - **Roland TR-8S**: 54 (tune, decay, level and CTRL for every instrument, plus FX)
  - **GENERIC**: standard controllers for any synth or your DAW's MIDI learn
- **Feels like the synth's own panel**: fine steps when you turn slowly, big ones on a flick, a click per option on switches, and walls at the ends of the range
- **Keys**: F1 for the next parameter (hold F1 and turn to pick from the list), F2 / F3 to step the synth's programs
- **Two ports at once**: class-compliant USB MIDI (no driver on Mac, Windows, Linux or iPad) and the 3.5 mm TRS jacks, so it plays a synth with no computer at all
- **Stays in sync**: if the synth sends its own knob moves back, the knob's value follows
- **On the knob**: KORG and Roland logos on the screen and on the idle screen, the value as a meter, and the LED ring filling with it

**Good to know**
- In MIDI mode the knob is a MIDI device instead of a keyboard and mouse, so switching into or out of MIDI reconnects USB for about a second
- Pick the synth and channel on the knob (F4 › Profiles › MIDI) or in the companion's Mode page
- New `tools/midi_monitor.html`: open it in Chrome to watch and decode everything the knob sends
