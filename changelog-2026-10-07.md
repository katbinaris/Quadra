**Quadra: the knob is the speaker 🔊**

The clicks now come from the motor itself. No speaker in the path: the knob you are holding is what makes the sound.

**What you get**
- **Louder, and it sounds like the knob**: every detent plays a short burst through the motor's own windings. On the two units tested it is louder than the old speaker, and a hand on the knob doesn't muffle it
- **Nine click sounds**: sine or square, short or long, some with a falling pitch, plus Tick, Ting and Tap. Pick one on the knob under **Haptics › Click**, where each is drawn as it sounds
- **A click per haptic profile**: WIDE, COARSE, MEDIUM, FINE and SMOOTH each keep their own, next to their volume and pitch. A coarse step can thump while a fine one ticks
- **Sound Cal** (F4 › Device › Sound Cal): the knob plays a sweep, you say what you heard, and it tunes the click to the pitch your knob rings at. A few minutes, hands off the knob
- **The startup chime** plays through the motor too
- **Device › Click** switches between two ways of making the sound: D rings the housing, Q shakes the knob

**Good to know**
- Run **Sound Cal** once after updating. Clicks work before that, at a default pitch
- Your tuned haptic profiles are kept. All five start with the same click; change them one by one and press F2
- Low click-volume settings are boosted, so quiet clicks stay audible
- The speaker and amplifier on current boards are no longer used. The firmware is about 20 KB smaller without them
- The companion's Haptics page has a **Click** picker too (Companion 0.3.0)

Sound is stepped 32,000 times a second, so clicks reach 10 kHz and Sound Cal sweeps 1 to 10 kHz.
